## 3. Theoretical Proof: The Information Value Theorem

Let $\Theta_t \in \mathcal{H} = \{\text{Aggressive}, \text{Moderate}, \text{Passive}\}$ denote the latent competitor posture, and let $O_t$ denote the censored auction feedback vector.

### Theorem (Information Value & Bellman Dominance)
Let $\mathcal{F}_t^0 = \sigma(R_0:t, I_0:t)$ be the filtration generated by the monopolistic ($N=0$) process, and let $\mathcal{F}_t^1 = \sigma(R_0:t, I_0:t, O_1:t)$ be the filtration generated by the competitive ($N=1$) MOMDP process with belief state $b_t = \mathbb{P}(\Theta_t \mid \mathcal{F}_t^1)$.

1. **Filtration Subordination:** For all decision epochs $t \ge 0$:
   $$\mathcal{F}_t^0 \subseteq \mathcal{F}_t^1$$
2. **Policy Set Inclusion:** Any $\mathcal{F}_t^0$-measurable policy is trivially $\mathcal{F}_t^1$-measurable:
   $$\Pi^{N=0} \subseteq \Pi^{N=1}$$
3. **Bellman Value Dominance:**
   $$V_t^{N=1}(S_t^{\text{ext}}) = \sup_{\pi \in \Pi^{N=1}} \mathbb{E}\left[ \sum_{\tau=t}^T \gamma^{\tau-t} C(S_\tau, a_\tau) \;\middle|\; \mathcal{F}_t^1 \right] \ge \sup_{\pi \in \Pi^{N=0}} \mathbb{E}\left[ \sum_{\tau=t}^T \gamma^{\tau-t} C(S_\tau, a_\tau) \;\middle|\; \mathcal{F}_t^0 \right] = V_t^{N=0}(S_t)$$
4. **Strict Dominance:** The inequality is strict whenever the observation channel has non-zero mutual information with latent competitor postures ($I(\Theta; O) > 0$) and optimal pricing bids depend on competitor posture:
   $$\exists \Theta_i \ne \Theta_j \quad \text{such that} \quad p^*(\Theta_i) \ne p^*(\Theta_j) \implies V_t^{N=1}(S_t^{\text{ext}}) > V_t^{N=0}(S_t)$$

*Proof:*
Because $\mathcal{F}_t^0 \subseteq \mathcal{F}_t^1$, the decision maker under $N=1$ optimizes over a strictly larger set of adapted policies. By the Law of Total Expectation and Jensen's inequality on the convex supremum operator:
$$\mathbb{E}\left[ \max_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \mathbb{E}[C(S, a, W) \mid \mathcal{F}_t^1] \;\middle|\; \mathcal{F}_t^0 \right] \ge \max_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \mathbb{E}[C(S, a, W) \mid \mathcal{F}_t^0]$$
The difference is the non-negative **Expected Value of Latent Information (EVLI)**:
$$\text{EVLI}_t = V_t^{N=1}(R_t, I_t, b_t) - V_t^{N=0}(R_t, I_t) \ge 0$$
$\blacksquare$
